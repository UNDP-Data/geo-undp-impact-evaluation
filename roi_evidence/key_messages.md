# Satellite Evidence on UNDP Early Recovery — Key Messages

* **UNDP can now use satellite data to show where early-recovery investments are associated with measurable economic recovery in crisis settings.** Nighttime lights provide an independent proxy for local economic activity, allowing UNDP to track recovery faster, more consistently, and at scale.

* **In Myanmar, roughly USD 16.8M across UNDP's active early-recovery portfolio is linked to an estimated USD 350–650M in additional local economic activity.** This implies an estimated **USD 20–40 in additional local economic activity for every USD 1 invested**, subject to confirmation of the underlying beneficiary count.

* **In Gaza, satellite evidence shows that employment and cash-for-work is the intervention type with the clearest measurable economic payoff.** It is the only intervention category with a statistically significant positive signal in nighttime lights.

## Robust impact evidence: Myanmar and Gaza

* **Myanmar — USD 20–40 in additional local economic activity for every USD 1 invested.** Using a robust difference-in-differences design, the analysis compares changes in nighttime lights before and after the 2025 earthquake in UNDP-supported areas against comparable non-UNDP areas. UNDP-supported areas recovered **30–55% more than comparable areas** (**p<0.001**), linking roughly **USD 16.8M across UNDP's active early-recovery portfolio in Myanmar** to an estimated **USD 350–650M** in additional local economic activity. National nighttime lights have returned to about **69%** of pre-earthquake levels.

* **Gaza — employment and cash-for-work show the clearest economic return.** Using a sector-level treated-versus-control satellite analysis by intervention type, the analysis finds that employment and cash-for-work is the only intervention type with a statistically significant positive signal, associated with a **+11.8% increase in nighttime lights**. On a conservative satellite-verified count of about **54,000 beneficiaries**, this returns roughly **USD 0.17 per USD 1 per year**, rising toward break-even — about **USD 1 per USD 1 invested** — once UNDP's full reported employment caseload is counted.

* **Gaza — debris removal and shelter are important, but their economic effects are not yet visible in the satellite data.** These interventions may be critical for restoring basic conditions, safety, and physical recovery, but they do not yet show a measurable increase in nighttime-light-based economic activity.

## Correlational recovery signals: additional country findings

* **Syria — full recovery alongside USD 98M in UNDP investment.** Nighttime lights have returned to approximately **100%** of pre-crisis levels. This is a promising recovery signal, but not yet a causal estimate of UNDP's impact.

* **Afghanistan — full recovery alongside USD 97M in UNDP investment.** Nighttime lights have returned to approximately **100%** of pre-crisis levels, suggesting substantial recovery in a difficult operating environment.

* **DR Congo — full recovery alongside USD 251M in UNDP investment.** Nighttime lights have returned to approximately **100%** of pre-crisis levels, providing a strong portfolio-level recovery signal.

* **Ukraine — partial recovery alongside USD 971M in UNDP investment, despite ongoing war.** Nighttime lights have recovered to approximately **48%** of pre-crisis levels. This should be framed as a resilience and partial-recovery signal, not a completed recovery story.

## Methodological notes for external use

* **Myanmar provides the strongest causal recovery estimate.** The **30–55%** recovery effect is directly estimated using a difference-in-differences design comparing UNDP-supported areas with comparable non-UNDP areas.

* **The Myanmar dollar return depends primarily on the beneficiary assumption.** The estimated **USD 350–650M** in additional local economic activity, and therefore the **USD 20–40 per USD 1 invested** figure, assumes approximately **3.5M beneficiaries** and **USD 1,122 GDP per capita**. The budget is sourced from the dashboard; the beneficiary count should be confirmed before the ROI figure is cited externally.

* **Gaza provides the strongest intervention-level signal.** The analysis identifies employment and cash-for-work as the only intervention type with a statistically significant positive economic effect in nighttime-light data. The return estimate depends on whether one uses the conservative satellite-verified beneficiary count or UNDP's full reported employment caseload.

* **Syria, Afghanistan, DR Congo, and Ukraine are correlational results.** These countries show recovery alongside UNDP investment, but further causal testing is needed before presenting these as attributable UNDP impact.

* **Nighttime lights are converted to GDP using a standard global elasticity of 0.3.** The estimates are strongest where satellite lights reliably capture economic activity, and should be interpreted more cautiously in very low-light or active-conflict settings.

* **Budgets are post-event project allocations from the dashboard and should be validated before external publication.**
